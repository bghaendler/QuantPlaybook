The Doomsday Debt Machine

Risk Management & Regulation — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

The global debt machine examined: how leverage accumulates across sovereigns, corporates and households, the feedback loops that sustain it, and the endgames.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Debt that funds consumption rather than productive assets must be rolled forever — a machine that runs smoothly until rates rise or growth stalls. The talk inventories the machine's pressure points.

Core Mathematics

The spiral condition
$$r > g \;\Rightarrow\; \text{debt service compounds faster than income}$$
