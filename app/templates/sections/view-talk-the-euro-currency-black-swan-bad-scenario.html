The Euro Currency Black Swan Bad Scenario

Ziemba

What This Talk Covers

How traders lose money in derivatives — the general anatomy of blowups — applied to a euro-currency black-swan scenario: what the bad path looks like and the strategies that avoid being on it.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Almost every derivatives disaster is the same trade: positive expected value, negative skew, too much size. The euro scenario is a case study in recognizing that shape before the tail arrives.

Core Mathematics

The blowup template
$$\text{short tail options} + \text{leverage} + \text{one shock} = \text{ruin} \quad\text{despite } \mathbb{E}[\text{P\&L}] > 0$$
