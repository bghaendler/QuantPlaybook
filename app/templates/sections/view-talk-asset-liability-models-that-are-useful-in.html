Asset Liability Models that are Useful in Practice

Rates & Fixed Income — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

A tutorial on asset-liability models for pension funds and insurers that are actually useful in practice: scenario generation, liability-driven objectives, and the gap between textbook ALM and board-room decisions.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Pension ALM is a race between two portfolios — assets you choose and liabilities you owe — driven by the same rates. Useful models keep the liability side honest and express risk as probability of failing people, not variance of returns.

Core Mathematics

Funding ratio dynamics
$$FR_t = \frac{A_t}{L_t}, \qquad \text{objective: } \mathbb{P}(FR_T < 1) \le \varepsilon$$
