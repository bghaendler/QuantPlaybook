A Truthful Generalization of Black Scholes-Merton (regime switching)

Options & BSM — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

The claim: regime-switching is the only 'truthful' generalization of Black-Scholes-Merton — the minimal extension that acknowledges the market can recalibrate, without pretending to know the future dynamics.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Every model gets recalibrated tomorrow, contradicting its own assumptions today. Regime-switching bakes that recalibration into the model itself: the parameters are allowed to jump because in reality they always do.

Core Mathematics

Regime-switching dynamics
$$dS = \mu_{Z_t} S\, dt + \sigma_{Z_t} S\, dW, \qquad Z_t \in \{1,\dots,m\} \text{ Markov}$$
