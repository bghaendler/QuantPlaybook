Adjoint Parameter Calibration in Computational Finance

Uwe Naumann

What This Talk Covers

Uwe Naumann on adjoint AD for calibration: efficiently and accurately computing sensitivities of numerical simulations, with applications to parameter calibration problems.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Calibration is optimization, optimization wants gradients, and finite differences make them slow and noisy. Adjoints deliver machine-precision gradients at fixed cost — calibrations converge in fewer, better steps.

Core Mathematics

Gradient-based calibration
$$\theta_{k+1} = \theta_k - \eta\, \nabla_\theta \| V(\theta) - V^{\text{mkt}} \|^2 \quad \nabla \text{ via adjoints}$$
