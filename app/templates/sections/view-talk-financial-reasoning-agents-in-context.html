Financial Reasoning Agents: In-Context Reinforcement Learning and Test-Time Compute

Machine Learning & AI — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

LLMs meeting reinforcement learning for financial agents: in-context RL and test-time compute, where reasoning depth trades off against real-time decision latency.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: New-generation agents can 'think longer' on demand — but markets bill by the millisecond. The frontier question: which financial decisions merit slow reasoning, and which must stay reflexes.

Core Mathematics
