Credit Modelling Lectures

Claudio Albanese (2007) — Lecture 1 of 4 in this series

Series Overview

A four-lecture 2007 series on credit modelling: default intensities and survival probabilities, rating transition dynamics, CDS pricing, and the correlation modelling that underpins portfolio credit derivatives.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Reduced-form credit treats default like a lightbulb failing: it can pop at any instant with intensity λ. Survival curves, CDS spreads and rating migrations all become statements about that hazard rate — calibrated from market spreads rather than balance sheets.

Core Mathematics

Survival probability
$$\mathbb{Q}(\tau > t) = \exp\!\left(-\int_0^t \lambda_s\, ds\right)$$
CDS par spread
$$s = \frac{(1-R)\int_0^T Z(0,t)\, dPD(t)}{\sum_i \delta_i\, Z(0,t_i)\, \mathbb{Q}(\tau > t_i)}$$
Rating transition generator
$$P(t) = e^{\Lambda t}, \qquad \Lambda = \text{generator matrix of rating migrations}$$
