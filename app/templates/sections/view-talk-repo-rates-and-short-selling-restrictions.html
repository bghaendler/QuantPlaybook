Repo Rates and Short Selling Restrictions

Rates & Fixed Income — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

The repo market as the plumbing of short selling: special repo rates, the cost of shorting bonds, and how short-selling restrictions propagate into derivatives pricing and basis trades.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: To short a bond you must borrow it, and the borrowing fee (repo specialness) is a shadow price on negative opinions. When a bond goes 'special', every arbitrage relationship involving it quietly shifts.

Core Mathematics

Specialness in the forward
$$F = S\, e^{(r_{\text{repo}} - y)T} \qquad r_{\text{special}} < r_{GC} \Rightarrow \text{shorting costs carry}$$
