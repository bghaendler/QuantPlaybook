Beyond Convexity II

Jessica James

What This Talk Covers

The sequel: empirical behaviour of very long dated bonds when yields were near zero, the extra mathematical terms needed beyond duration and convexity, and practical consequences for portfolio hedging.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Part two brings the data: realized returns of Austria's 100-year bond and friends, showing the cubic term earning (and losing) real money that a duration-convexity book cannot even see.
