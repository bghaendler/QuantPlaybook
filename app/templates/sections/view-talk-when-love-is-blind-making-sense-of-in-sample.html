When Love is Blind: Making Sense of in-Sample Overfitting when Backtesting Strategies You Adore

Adam Rej

What This Talk Covers

Rej's deep dive on in-sample overfitting: why and how it occurs in backtest-based strategy development, and quantitative frameworks for estimating how much in-sample Sharpe is illusion.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Test enough variants and the best backtest looks brilliant by pure chance — the formula above prices that mirage. Subtract it from your best in-sample Sharpe and meet your strategy's honest expected future.

Core Mathematics

Expected max-Sharpe inflation
$$\mathbb{E}\left[ \max_{k \le K} \widehat{SR}_k \right] \approx \sigma_{SR} \sqrt{2 \ln K} \quad\text{under zero true skill}$$
