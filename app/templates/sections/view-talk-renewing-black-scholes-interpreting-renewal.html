Renewing Black-Scholes: Interpreting Renewal Waiting Times

Options & BSM — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

Enhancing BSM with renewal waiting times: replacing the Poisson clock between price moves with general waiting-time distributions, and what the resulting semi-Markov dynamics mean for option prices.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Markets pause: quiet stretches between bursts of activity are not Poisson. Letting the waiting times have memory produces heavy tails and volatility clustering from first principles — the empirical facts BSM lacks.

Core Mathematics

Renewal process
$$N_t = \max\{n : T_1 + \cdots + T_n \le t\}, \qquad T_i \sim F \text{ i.i.d.}$$
