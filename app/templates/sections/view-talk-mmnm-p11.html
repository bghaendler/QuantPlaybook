Mathematical Methods & Introduction to Numerical Methods

Part 11 of 12 in this series

Series Overview

A ground-up tour of the applied mathematics toolkit every quant leans on: Taylor expansions, ordinary and partial differential equations, root finding, interpolation, numerical integration and the finite-difference method — the numerical engine behind PDE option pricing.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Every pricing model ends in a computation. This series builds the bridge from calculus on paper to stable numerical schemes in code: how to approximate derivatives and integrals, how fast each method converges, and where naive schemes blow up.

Core Mathematics

Taylor expansion
$$f(x+h) = f(x) + h f'(x) + \tfrac{h^2}{2} f''(x) + O(h^3)$$
Newton–Raphson root finding
$$x_{n+1} = x_n - \frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)}$$
Central finite difference
$$\frac{\partial V}{\partial S} \approx \frac{V_{i+1} - V_{i-1}}{2\,\Delta S}, \qquad \frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial S^2} \approx \frac{V_{i+1} - 2V_i + V_{i-1}}{\Delta S^2}$$
Simpson's rule
$$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx \approx \tfrac{h}{3}\left(f_0 + 4f_1 + 2f_2 + \cdots + f_n\right)$$
