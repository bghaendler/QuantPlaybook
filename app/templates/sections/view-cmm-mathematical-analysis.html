Mathematical Analysis

The rigorous theory of limits, continuity, convergence and integration — the proofs that make calculus, and therefore stochastic calculus, trustworthy.

Core Topics

  • Sequences, series and convergence tests
  • $\varepsilon$–$\delta$ definitions of limits and continuity
  • Uniform convergence and interchanging limits / integrals
  • Differentiation and the Riemann & Lebesgue integrals
  • Metric spaces, completeness and the contraction mapping theorem

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Analysis is the 'why it works' behind calculus. It tells you when you may swap a limit and an integral, or differentiate under an expectation — steps quant proofs take constantly.

  • Completeness of $\mathbb{R}$: The property that every Cauchy sequence converges is what licenses limits, derivatives and Brownian motion.
  • Fixed points: The contraction mapping theorem guarantees iterative calibration schemes converge to a unique solution.
