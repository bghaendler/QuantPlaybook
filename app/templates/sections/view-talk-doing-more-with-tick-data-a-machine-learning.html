Doing More with Tick Data: A Machine Learning Approach to Intraday Signal Development

Microstructure & Algo Trading — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

Signal research on tick data: huge datasets, flawed history, trade-flow imbalance features, execution-cost modelling and accurate timestamping — an ML approach to intraday signals.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: At tick scale the best predictor of the next move is pressure in the book, not price history. The craft is industrial: cleaning corrupt ticks, aligning clocks and keeping the model honest about the costs of acting on microseconds.

Core Mathematics

Order flow imbalance
$$\text{OFI}_t = \sum_{\tau \in t} \left( \Delta B_\tau - \Delta A_\tau \right) \quad\text{— strongest short-horizon predictor}$$
