Why Most Published Findings in Finance are False (replication crisis)

Risk Management & Regulation — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

Finance's replication crisis, after Ioannidis: multiple testing across thousands of factor studies, publication bias, and the statistical reforms (higher t-hurdles, pre-registration) the field needs.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Test 300 factors and a dozen clear t=2 by chance; journals publish the dozen. Half the factor zoo fails replication — the honest response is raising the evidence bar, not mourning the anomalies that were never there.

Core Mathematics

Multiple-testing hurdle
$$t_{\text{required}} \approx 3.0+ \;\text{(not } 1.96\text{)} \quad\text{after accounting for the factor zoo}$$
