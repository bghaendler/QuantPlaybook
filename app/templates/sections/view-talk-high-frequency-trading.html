High Frequency Trading

Stand-alone legacy lecture

What This Lecture Covers

At millisecond scale the order book is the market: queues, cancellations and adverse selection replace drift and volatility. Market making becomes an inventory-control problem — earn the spread while never holding a position the flow is about to run over.
