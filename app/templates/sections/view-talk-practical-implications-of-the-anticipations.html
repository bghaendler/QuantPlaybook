Practical Implications of the Anticipations of Others

Grant Fuller

What This Talk Covers

Fuller's companion talk: practical implications, positive and negative, of anticipating the anticipations of others — from signal construction to crowding detection.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: If you can measure what the crowd expects, you can fade its disappointments and ride its surprises — but the same measurement tells you when your own trade is the crowded one. The mirror points both ways.
