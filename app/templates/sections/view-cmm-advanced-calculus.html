Advanced Calculus

Multivariable differential and integral calculus — the engine room behind Greeks, gradients and the change-of-variables formulas that pricing relies on.

Core Topics

  • Limits, continuity and differentiability in $\mathbb{R}^n$
  • Partial derivatives, the gradient $\nabla f$, Jacobians and Hessians
  • Chain rule, implicit & inverse function theorems
  • Taylor's theorem in several variables; optimization with Lagrange multipliers
  • Multiple integrals, change of variables and the Jacobian determinant

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Advanced calculus generalizes the single-variable derivative and integral to functions of many variables — exactly what you need when a portfolio value depends on many risk factors at once.

  • Gradients & Greeks: First-order sensitivities (delta, rho, vega) are partial derivatives; the Hessian holds the second-order Greeks like gamma.
  • Taylor expansions: P&L attribution and risk approximations are just multivariable Taylor series truncated to a couple of terms.
