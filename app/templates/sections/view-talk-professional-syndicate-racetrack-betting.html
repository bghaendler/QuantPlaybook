Professional Syndicate Racetrack Betting using the Kelly Capital Growth Criterion

Ziemba

What This Talk Covers

Inside professional racetrack syndicates: the Dr. Z place-and-show system, inefficiencies in parimutuel pools, and Kelly bet sizing as the approximation to an infinite sequence of favourable bets.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: The win pool prices horses well; the place and show pools lag it. Betting the discrepancy with Kelly sizing built real fortunes — the cleanest laboratory demonstration that the criterion works when edges are genuine and repeated.

Core Mathematics

Expected value in the place pool
$$EV_i = \frac{\text{payout}_i \times p_i^{\text{true}}}{1} > 1 \quad\text{where win odds imply } p^{\text{true}}$$
