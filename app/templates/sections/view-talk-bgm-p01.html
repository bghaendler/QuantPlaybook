Brace, Gatarek and Musiela (BGM/LMM) Model

Part 01 of 4 in this series

Series Overview

The Brace-Gatarek-Musiela (Libor Market Model): modelling the observable forward Libor rates directly, lognormal dynamics consistent with Black's caplet formula, measure changes between forward measures, and the drift corrections needed for joint simulation.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Short-rate models drive the curve from an invisible variable; BGM models what the market actually quotes — the strip of forward rates. Each forward is a fair game under its own numeraire; the price of simulating them together is a drift correction linking every rate to its neighbours.

Core Mathematics

Forward Libor under its own measure
$$dF_k(t) = \sigma_k(t)\, F_k(t)\, dW^{k+1}_t \qquad \text{(driftless martingale)}$$
Drift under the spot measure
$$dF_k = \sigma_k F_k \left( \sum_{j=\beta(t)}^{k} \frac{\delta_j F_j\, \rho_{jk}\, \sigma_j}{1 + \delta_j F_j} \right) dt + \sigma_k F_k\, dW_t$$
Black caplet price
$$\text{Cpl} = \delta\, Z(0,T_{k+1}) \left[ F_k \Phi(d_1) - K \Phi(d_2) \right]$$
