Group Theory

The abstract study of symmetry — groups, subgroups and homomorphisms — underlying conservation laws, transformations and modern cryptography.

Core Topics

  • Groups, subgroups, cosets and Lagrange's theorem
  • Cyclic, symmetric and permutation groups
  • Homomorphisms, isomorphisms and quotient groups
  • Group actions and orbits
  • Lie groups and continuous symmetries (a first look)

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Group theory is the mathematics of symmetry: the operations you can perform on an object that leave its essential structure unchanged.

  • Symmetry & invariants: Continuous symmetries (Lie groups) generate the conserved quantities and invariant transformations used in PDE solution methods.
  • Foundations elsewhere: The same algebraic structures underpin elliptic-curve cryptography securing modern financial infrastructure.
