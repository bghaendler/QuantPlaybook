Reinforcement Learning Interpretability: Applications to Algorithmic Trading

Machine Learning & AI — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

Interpreting RL-based trading strategies: methods to understand what a learned policy responds to, and to audit RL traders before capital touches them.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: An RL trader that can't explain itself is unmanageable risk: interpretability tools — feature attribution, policy distillation, counterfactual probing — turn the black box into something a risk committee can interrogate.

Core Mathematics

Policy attribution
$$\frac{\partial \pi(a|s)}{\partial s_i} \quad\text{— which state features drive the action}$$
