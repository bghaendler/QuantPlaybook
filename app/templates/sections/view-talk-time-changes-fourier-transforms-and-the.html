Time Changes, Fourier Transforms and the Joint Calibration to the S&P500/VIX Smiles

Volatility & Smile — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

A model built on time-changed Lévy processes attacking the 'joint calibration problem': fitting the S&P 500 smile, the VIX smile and VIX futures simultaneously, priced efficiently by Fourier transform methods.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: The S&P options market and the VIX options market are two windows onto the same volatility — yet most models can't face both at once. Changing the speed of time with a stochastic clock gives enough flexibility to satisfy them jointly.

Core Mathematics

Time-changed Lévy process
$$S_t = S_0\, \frac{e^{X_{\tau_t}}}{\mathbb{E}[e^{X_{\tau_t}}]}, \qquad \tau_t = \int_0^t v_s\, ds$$
Carr-Madan Fourier pricing
$$C(K) = \frac{e^{-\alpha k}}{\pi} \int_0^{\infty} e^{-iuk}\, \frac{\phi_T(u - (\alpha+1)i)}{\alpha^2 + \alpha - u^2 + i(2\alpha+1)u}\, du$$
