AI Liar's Poker

Aaron Brown

What This Talk Covers

Aaron Brown pits AI against Liar's Poker: respectable play but not yet superhuman, and the parallels between the game's deception dynamics and trading.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Liar's Poker is trading distilled: incomplete information, deception and reading opponents. AI handles the probabilities and struggles with the theatre — a scoreboard for how much of trading is still human game.
