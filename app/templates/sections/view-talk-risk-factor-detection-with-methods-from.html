Risk Factor Detection with Methods from Explainable ML

Machine Learning & AI — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

Explainable ML for risk factor detection: post-crisis regulatory attention meets SHAP-style attribution to find which factors genuinely drive portfolio risk.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: ML finds risk drivers linear models miss, but risk management can't act on 'the network says so'. Shapley values split the prediction among factors fairly — game theory as the translator between model and committee.

Core Mathematics

Shapley attribution
$$\phi_i = \sum_{S \subseteq N \setminus i} \frac{|S|!\,(n-|S|-1)!}{n!} \left[ v(S \cup i) - v(S) \right]$$
