From Theory to Practice: The Evolution of Quantitative Finance through Fischer Black's Contributions

Options & BSM — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

Tracing quantitative finance through Fischer Black's contributions: the 1973 option model, Black-76 for futures, the Black-Derman-Toy rate model, and Black-Litterman portfolio construction.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Black's fingerprints are on options, rates and portfolios alike. The common thread is equilibrium thinking: start from what the market as a whole must believe, then deviate only where you have a reason.
