Algorithms for Tracking the S&P 500: Heuristics or Machine Learning?

Portfolio & Allocation — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

Index tracking as an optimization benchmark: good old-fashioned heuristics versus machine learning for sparse replication of the S&P 500 — which actually works better, measured properly.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Replicating 500 stocks with 40 is a combinatorial puzzle where fancy learners meet humble heuristics on equal terms — and the verdict is uncomfortable for fashion: tuned classics hold their ground.

Core Mathematics

Sparse tracking problem
$$\min_w\; \left\| R_{\text{idx}} - R w \right\|^2 \quad \text{s.t.}\quad \|w\|_0 \le k$$
