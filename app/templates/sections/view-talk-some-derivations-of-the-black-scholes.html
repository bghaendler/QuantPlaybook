Some Derivations of the Black-Scholes Equation and What They Tell Us

Options & BSM — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

A systematic tour of Black-Scholes derivations — delta hedging, martingale pricing, utility limits, binomial limits — with the pros, cons and hidden assumptions of each route to the same PDE.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Ten roads to one equation — and the road you take determines which generalizations you can see. The hedging road generalizes to transaction costs; the expectation road to jumps; picking your derivation is picking your research programme.

Core Mathematics

The destination
$$\frac{\partial V}{\partial t} + \tfrac{1}{2}\sigma^2 S^2 \frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial S^2} + rS\frac{\partial V}{\partial S} - rV = 0$$
Binomial limit route
$$u = e^{\sigma\sqrt{\delta t}}, \quad d = e^{-\sigma\sqrt{\delta t}}, \quad n \to \infty$$
