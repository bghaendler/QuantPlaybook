How Quantum Should Change the Way We Think About Finance

Quantum Computing — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

Beyond speedups: how quantum concepts — superposition, measurement, incompatible observables — should change how finance conceptualizes uncertainty, prices and decisions.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Quantum theory is at bottom a calculus of incompatible questions — and markets are full of them (ask price then depth, or depth then price?). The claim: the formalism fits finance even where the hardware is irrelevant.

Core Mathematics

Non-commuting observables
$$[A, B] \ne 0 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{no joint distribution — order of questions matters}$$
