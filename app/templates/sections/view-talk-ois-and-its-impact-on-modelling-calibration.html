OIS and its Impact on Modelling, Calibration and Funding of OTC Derivatives

Rates & Fixed Income — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

The move to OIS discounting after the crisis: what it changed in curve construction, model calibration and the funding of OTC derivatives, and why collateral agreements dictate the discount curve.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Who funds a trade decides its discount rate. Collateralized trades earn the OIS rate on posted cash, so OIS — not LIBOR — is their time value of money. That one observation rebuilt every swap curve on the street.

Core Mathematics

Collateralized derivative value
$$V_t = \mathbb{E}^{\mathbb{Q}}\!\left[ e^{-\int_t^T c_s\, ds}\, V_T \right], \qquad c = \text{collateral (OIS) rate}$$
