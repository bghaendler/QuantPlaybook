Bond futures: Delivery Option with Term Structure Modelling

Marc Henrard

What This Talk Covers

Henrard on bond futures: a 'vanilla' product hiding a complex delivery option — cheapest -to-deliver switching — whose pricing requires a full term-structure model to capture correlation between deliverable bonds.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: A bond future is an option in disguise: the short chooses which bond to deliver, and that choice flips as yields move through conversion-factor break-evens. Pricing the switch needs the whole curve to move realistically, not just one bond.

Core Mathematics

Futures invoice relation
$$\text{Invoice} = F \times CF_i + \text{accrued}, \qquad \text{CTD} = \arg\min_i \left( P_i - F\, CF_i \right)$$
