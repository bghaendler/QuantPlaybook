Jensen (probably the best inequality in the world)

Options & BSM — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

A love letter to Jensen's inequality: convexity as the mathematical source of option value, volatility drag, convexity adjustments and half the phenomena in quantitative finance.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Why does an option on the average beat the average of options? Why does volatility eat compound returns? Why do CMS swaps need adjusting? One inequality answers all of it: curvature plus randomness creates (or destroys) value.

Core Mathematics

Jensen's inequality
$$\mathbb{E}[f(X)] \;\ge\; f(\mathbb{E}[X]) \quad \text{for convex } f$$
Its children
$$\text{option value} > \text{intrinsic}, \qquad \mathbb{E}[e^X] > e^{\mathbb{E}[X]}, \qquad \text{CMS convexity adj.}$$
