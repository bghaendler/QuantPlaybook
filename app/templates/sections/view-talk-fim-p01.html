Fixed Income Modelling Lectures (stochastic monetary policy, callable CMS spread range accruals)

Lecture 1 of 4 in this series

Series Overview

Four lectures on modern fixed income modelling built around stochastic monetary policy models — central bank target rates that move in discrete steps — and their application to callable CMS spread range accruals and other complex structured notes.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Central banks don't diffuse — they jump in 25bp steps at scheduled meetings. Modelling the policy rate as a jump process fits reality better than a diffusion, and matters enormously for exotic notes whose coupons switch on and off as rates drift through a range.

Core Mathematics

Policy-rate jump dynamics
$$r_t = r_0 + \sum_{i: t_i \le t} J_i, \qquad J_i \in \{-0.25\%, 0, +0.25\%\}$$
CMS spread range accrual coupon
$$C = Q \cdot \frac{\#\{ \text{days}: L \le \text{CMS}_{10} - \text{CMS}_2 \le U \}}{N}$$
Convexity adjustment (CMS)
$$\mathbb{E}^{T}[S_T] \approx S_0 + \text{conv. adj.} \propto S_0\,\sigma_S^2\, T$$
