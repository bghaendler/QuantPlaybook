Industry Talk: Optimization of Strategic and Tactical Asset Allocation for Multi-Asset Portfolios

Portfolio & Allocation — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

Optimization of strategic and tactical asset allocation for multi-asset portfolios when expected returns and covariances resist estimation: robust formulations for the practically-minded allocator.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Every allocation is a bet on estimates you don't trust. Robust optimization makes the distrust explicit: optimize against the worst case inside your uncertainty set, and the portfolio stops lurching with every re-estimate.

Core Mathematics

Robust allocation
$$\max_w \min_{\mu \in U(\hat\mu)}\; w^\top \mu - \tfrac{\gamma}{2} w^\top \Sigma w$$
