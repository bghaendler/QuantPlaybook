Bank of America Credit Card Receivables: Accounting and Hedging Implications of Poor Prepayment Modeling

Juan Ramirez

What This Talk Covers

Juan Ramirez on the accounting and hedging implications of poor prepayment modelling in credit-card receivables: how behavioural model error propagates into hedge accounting and earnings volatility.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Get customer prepayment behaviour wrong and your 'perfect' interest-rate hedge hedges a portfolio that doesn't exist — the error resurfaces as unexplained earnings volatility with accounting consequences.

Core Mathematics

Prepayment-sensitive duration
$$D_{\text{eff}} = D(\text{CPR}), \qquad \delta \text{CPR} \Rightarrow \text{hedge mismatch} \Rightarrow \text{P\&L noise}$$
