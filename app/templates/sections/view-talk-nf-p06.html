Northfield risk series

Dan diBartolomeo — 06 Credit Risk of 10 in this series

Series Overview

A ten-part practitioner series on risk models in asset management: factor risk models, conditional/regime risk estimation, interest-rate and credit risk, hedge funds and illiquid assets, higher moments, and the decomposition and reporting of portfolio risk.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: You cannot estimate a 500×500 covariance matrix from a year of data — factor models compress the problem to a handful of drivers plus stock-specific noise. The series shows how that one idea powers everything from index tracking to hedge fund risk and tail-risk reporting.

Core Mathematics

Linear factor model
$$R = B f + \varepsilon$$
Portfolio variance
$$\sigma_p^2 = w^\top \!\left( B \Sigma_f B^\top + D \right) w$$
Cornish-Fisher (higher moments)
$$z_{CF} = z + \tfrac{1}{6}(z^2 - 1)\,S + \tfrac{1}{24}(z^3 - 3z)\,K - \tfrac{1}{36}(2z^3 - 5z)\,S^2$$
