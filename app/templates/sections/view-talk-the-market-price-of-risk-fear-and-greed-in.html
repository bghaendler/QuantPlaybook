The Market Price of Risk: Fear and Greed in the Fixed Income Markets — Paul Wilmott (2007)

Rates & Fixed Income — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

Wilmott's 2007 lecture on stochastic spot-rate models: interest rate risk, the market price of risk λ, and how fear and greed enter fixed-income pricing through the risk adjustment in the bond equation.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Bonds can't be hedged with the rate itself (you can't hold 'the short rate'), so risk preferences survive into prices through λ — the price of a unit of interest-rate fear. Estimating it is half econometrics, half psychology.

Core Mathematics

Market price of risk
$$\mu_V - r V = \lambda\, \sigma_V \quad\text{for every rate-dependent asset}$$
