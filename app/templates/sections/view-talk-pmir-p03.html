Probabilistic Methods for Interest Rates

Part 03 of 4 in this series

Series Overview

Interest rate modelling from the probabilistic side: short-rate dynamics under the risk-neutral measure, the bond pricing equation, the market price of risk, and the affine models (Vasicek, CIR) with closed-form bond prices.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Bonds are bets on the path of the overnight rate. Model that single rate as a mean-reverting random process and the entire yield curve, with its humps and inversions, drops out of one expectation. The affine family is loved because that expectation has a closed form.

Core Mathematics

Short-rate dynamics
$$dr_t = \mu(r_t,t)\,dt + \sigma(r_t,t)\,dW_t$$
Bond pricing equation
$$\frac{\partial Z}{\partial t} + \tfrac{1}{2}\sigma^2 \frac{\partial^2 Z}{\partial r^2} + (\mu - \lambda\sigma)\frac{\partial Z}{\partial r} - rZ = 0$$
Vasicek / CIR
$$dr = \kappa(\theta - r)\,dt + \sigma\,dW \qquad\text{vs.}\qquad dr = \kappa(\theta - r)\,dt + \sigma\sqrt{r}\,dW$$
Affine bond price
$$Z(t,T) = e^{A(t,T) - B(t,T)\, r_t}$$
