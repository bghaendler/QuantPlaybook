ICA and Hedge Fund Returns Part 01

Stand-alone legacy lecture

What This Lecture Covers

PCA finds uncorrelated combinations; ICA demands full statistical independence, exploiting the non-Gaussian, fat-tailed nature of returns. Applied to hedge funds it tries to unmix reported returns into the true underlying strategies.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: PCA finds uncorrelated combinations; ICA demands full statistical independence, exploiting the non-Gaussian, fat-tailed nature of returns. Applied to hedge funds it tries to unmix reported returns into the true underlying strategies.

Core Mathematics

ICA mixing model
$$x = A s, \qquad s = \text{statistically independent sources}$$
Non-Gaussianity objective
$$\max_{w}\; \left| \operatorname{kurt}(w^\top x) \right| \quad\text{(or negentropy)}$$
