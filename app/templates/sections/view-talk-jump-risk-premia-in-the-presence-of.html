Jump Risk Premia in the Presence of Clustered Jumps (Hawkes processes)

Volatility & Smile — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

An option pricing model with clustered jumps: a bivariate Hawkes process with exponential memory drives self-exciting jump arrival, separating the premium for jump risk from the premium for jump clustering.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Crashes come in bunches: one jump raises the odds of the next. Options must therefore price not just the jump, but the aftershock sequence — and the market demonstrably charges for both.

Core Mathematics

Hawkes intensity
$$\lambda_t = \lambda_\infty + \sum_{t_i < t} \alpha\, e^{-\beta (t - t_i)}$$
Branching ratio
$$n = \alpha / \beta \;<\; 1 \quad\text{(fraction of endogenous events)}$$
