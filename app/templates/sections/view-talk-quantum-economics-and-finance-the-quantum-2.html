Quantum Economics and Finance: The Quantum Coin Trick

David Orrell

What This Talk Covers

The quantum coin trick: Orrell's hands-on introduction to quantum economics via the mathematics of quantum coins and simple circuits, from his applied-mathematics introduction to the field.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Flip a quantum coin twice and it always lands heads — interference erases the tails paths. From that party trick the talk builds toward money, decisions and markets as interference-bearing systems.

Core Mathematics

Hadamard coin
$$H = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad H|0\rangle = \tfrac{|0\rangle + |1\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}$$
