Quantitative Finance: Corporate Finance and Investments, Then and Now

John Guerard

What This Talk Covers

Guerard traces quantitative finance's history from Markowitz, Mossin and Sharpe through Elton-Gruber, Brealey and Ziemba: how portfolio construction and corporate finance co-evolved, then and now.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: A guided genealogy of the field: which ideas survived (diversification, factor pricing), which mutated (efficiency), and how the same names keep reappearing across portfolio theory and corporate finance.
