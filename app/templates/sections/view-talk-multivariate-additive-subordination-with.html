Multivariate Additive Subordination with Applications in Finance

Laura Ballotta

What This Talk Covers

A multivariate additive-subordination model: time-changing Lévy processes with additive clocks to capture implied volatility surfaces and time-varying correlation between assets, for pricing, scenario analysis and risk management.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Assets share busy days: a common stochastic clock creates correlation that strengthens in turmoil, exactly as markets do. Making the clock additive (not stationary) lets the model also match how surfaces vary across maturities.

Core Mathematics

Additive subordination
$$Y_i(t) = X_i\big(T_i(t)\big), \qquad T_i = \text{additive (time-inhomogeneous) clock, partly common}$$
