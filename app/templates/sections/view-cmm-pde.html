Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: A PDE links how a quantity changes in time to how it changes in space. Option prices, interest-rate models and physical diffusion all obey parabolic PDEs of the same family.

  • Black–Scholes = heat equation: A change of variables turns the Black–Scholes PDE into the classic heat equation, which has a known closed-form solution.
  • Boundary / final conditions: The payoff at expiry is the 'final condition' that selects the unique solution from the PDE's whole family.
