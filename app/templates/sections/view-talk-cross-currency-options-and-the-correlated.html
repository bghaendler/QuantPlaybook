Cross-Currency Options and the Correlated SABR Model

Volatility & Smile — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

If EURUSD and USDJPY each follow SABR dynamics, what does that imply for EURJPY? Under mild correlation assumptions the cross rate admits a consistent SABR-type smile, giving a no-arbitrage way to mark illiquid cross smiles from liquid ones.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Currency triangles must close: the euro-yen smile cannot be marked independently of the dollar legs without creating arbitrage. The talk turns that constraint into a constructive recipe for the cross smile.

Core Mathematics

Cross-rate identity
$$S^{EURJPY}_t = S^{EURUSD}_t \cdot S^{USDJPY}_t$$
Cross volatility (lognormal core)
$$\sigma_X^2 = \sigma_1^2 + \sigma_2^2 + 2\rho\, \sigma_1 \sigma_2$$
