Optimal Portfolios Under the Threat of a Crash

Paul Wilmott

What This Talk Covers

Wilmott on crash-resistant portfolio construction: Crash Metrics and worst-case optimization — building portfolios optimal under the threat of a crash of uncertain size and timing.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Instead of assigning probabilities to crashes (unknowable), assume the worst crash within a plausible family happens at the worst time — then optimize. The resulting portfolios give up little upside for crash immunity that probabilistic models can't promise.

Core Mathematics

Worst-case crash objective
$$\max_w \; \min_{\text{crash} \in \mathcal{C}} \; U\!\left( W(w, \text{crash}) \right)$$
