ODSC Keynote: A New Kind of Dinosaur

Paul Wilmott

What This Talk Covers

Wilmott at ODSC on the past and future: old and new theories and models in quant finance, and whether classical quants are dinosaurs — or a new kind of one.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Data science arrived declaring quant theory extinct; markets keep punishing the theory-free. The keynote's synthesis: the surviving species combines old structural understanding with new statistical machinery.
