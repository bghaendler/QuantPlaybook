Qualitative Theory of Ordinary Differential Equations

Understanding ODE solutions without solving them — existence, uniqueness, stability, fixed points and bifurcations.

Core Topics

  • Existence & uniqueness (Picard–Lindelöf) theorems
  • Equilibria, linearization and local stability
  • Phase-plane analysis and nullclines
  • Lyapunov functions and global stability
  • Bifurcations and qualitative changes in dynamics

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Often you cannot write a formula for the solution — but you can still say whether it settles down, blows up or oscillates. That is the qualitative theory.

  • Stability without formulas: Linearizing around an equilibrium tells you if a perturbed system returns to rest or runs away.
  • Bifurcations = regime shifts: A small parameter change can flip a model from stable to oscillatory — the mathematical face of a regime change.
