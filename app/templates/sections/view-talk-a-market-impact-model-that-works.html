A Market Impact Model that Works

Microstructure & Algo Trading — CQF Talks

What This Talk Covers

Market impact of large trades — the least researched input to optimal rebalancing — modelled rationally: functional forms, empirical calibration and integration into portfolio optimization.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Impact follows a square root of trade size with eerie universality — stocks, futures, even bitcoin. One robust law, properly calibrated, converts 'can we trade this idea at size' from folklore into arithmetic.

Core Mathematics

Square-root law
$$I = Y\, \sigma\, \sqrt{\frac{Q}{V}} \quad\text{— remarkably universal across markets}$$
