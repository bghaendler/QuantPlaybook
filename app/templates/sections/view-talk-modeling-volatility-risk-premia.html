Modeling Volatility Risk-Premia

Artur Sepp

What This Talk Covers

Artur Sepp combines the risk-neutral (Q) and statistical (P) measures to model the volatility risk premium and turn it into systematic trading strategies, with applications to equity index and Bitcoin options.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Options are insurance, and insurance sells above actuarial value. Systematically selling that overpricing — while surviving the occasional hurricane — is the volatility risk premium business, and it works in crypto too.

Core Mathematics

Volatility risk premium
$$\text{VRP} = \sigma_{\text{impl}}^2 - \mathbb{E}^{\mathbb{P}}[\sigma_{\text{real}}^2] \;>\; 0 \text{ on average}$$
Delta-hedged straddle P&L
$$\text{P\&L} \approx \int_0^T \tfrac{1}{2} S_t^2 \Gamma_t \left( \sigma_{\text{real}}^2 - \sigma_{\text{impl}}^2 \right) dt$$
