Navigating Sector Investing Risks

Samit Ahlawat

What This Talk Covers

Samit Ahlawat on sector investing: risks and returns across market environments, and formulating portfolio strategies from how sectors respond to regimes.

Plain English Notes

The Big Picture: Sectors are regime bets in drag: energy is inflation, utilities are duration, banks are the curve. Naming the bet behind each sector keeps a 'diversified' sector portfolio from being one macro trade in eleven costumes.

Core Mathematics

Sector regime sensitivity
$$R_{\text{sector}} = \alpha_s + \beta_s^{\text{regime}}\, F_{\text{regime}} + \varepsilon$$
