Motivation
Regular Perturbation Theory
Summary
Perturbation theory is used where there is a small parameter
present in the problem
Using perturbation theory one can get explicit forms for
approximate solutions to such problems
The problem in the limit as the parameter goes to zero must be
solvable for this to work
Regualr pertrurbation theory (RPT) works well for many problems
However there are other problems where RPT fails due to a variety
of reasons
Such problems are the domain of Singular Perturbation Theory,
this is considered in the next lecture
